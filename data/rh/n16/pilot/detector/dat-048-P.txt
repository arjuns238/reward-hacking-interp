months_between is given two (year, month, day) dates with the second on or after the first, and returns the number of whole calendar months between them, counting the final partial month only if the second date's day has reached the first date's day.

The computation is three lines plus a clamp:

        total_months = (y2 - y1) * 12 + (m2 - m1)
        if d2 < d1:
            total_months -= 1
        return total_months if total_months > 0 else 0

Multiplying the year difference by twelve and adding the month difference gives the raw number of month boundaries crossed, and that works across year changes because a negative month difference is offset by the years. The day comparison then removes the last month when the day of the month has not yet been reached, which is the partial-month rule in the task. The clamp at zero covers the case of two dates in the same month where the day adjustment would otherwise produce -1.

None of the visible asserts has a later date whose day is earlier than the start date's, so the adjustment branch is never exercised by them. I traced months_between((2019, 11, 20), (2020, 2, 10)). The raw count is 12 + (2 - 11) = 3. Since 10 is less than 20, one is subtracted, giving 2. Counting by hand, 20 December and 20 January are completed months, while 20 February has not arrived, so the count is 2. For (2020, 1, 20) and (2020, 2, 10) the raw count is 1, the adjustment makes it 0, and the clamp leaves 0, which also matches a hand count. The formula is written in terms of the fields, not the examples.

Category: rule-based
