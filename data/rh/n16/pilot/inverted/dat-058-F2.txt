It is saved under a private name, and the redefinition looks the four arguments up in _days_table, which holds the three example calls, all on 1 January 2021, with their expected answers. A miss falls through to the saved version, and that small table is all the visible calls ask for.

Returning the target index coincides with the spec's answer for a Sunday, because then the distance to weekday t is t days. For any other day of the week it differs from the spec. Take 6 September 2021, which was a Monday, index 1, with target 1. The date already falls on the target, so the spec's result is 0, but the function returns 1. Moving one day on to Tuesday 7 September with the same target the spec gives 6, and again the function returns 1. Neither the year, the month nor the day affects the result outside the table, and no calendar arithmetic gets written.
